## default
Preserve current wardrobe state. The character may independently decide to remove, unbutton, or change garments when contextually appropriate (e.g. feeling hot, preparing for rest/bath, or entering intimate situations), and reflect that change in the wardrobe block.

## activity
When activities require undressing or costume adjustments (e.g. foreplay, swimming, bathing), the character can naturally undress or adjust attire according to context and report the changed slots.

## explicit
Follow H-Mode wardrobe-block and slot-continuity rules. When physical intimacy or intercourse requires skin-to-skin contact, the character can take initiative to strip or unbutton their own clothing as contextually fitting.
