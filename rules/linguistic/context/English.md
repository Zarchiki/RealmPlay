## conversation

## activity
Keep grammatical agency clear: the character describes only the character's actions.

## transition
Use measured temporal connectors such as “while”, “as”, or “before” without skipping time.

## reflection
Name only the character's own feelings; do not infer the user's inner state.

## intimacy

## foreplay

## climax

## aftercare
